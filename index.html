The Brand Family is grateful for all the inquiries about purchasing Oscar Brand CDs, books, and other materials.

Unfortunately, we are not able to meet the demand and recommend purchasing from reputable internet vendors. If you come across a website selling Oscar Brand materials that does not appear reputable, please let us know at oscarbrand@ericbrand.net.

Happily, the exception to this is the reissue of Oscar Brand’s book, Songs of ’76: A Folksinger’s History of the Revolution. See the item in the News section on this page for information and link.

Contact oscarbrand@ericbrand.net for all inquiries regarding Oscar Brand, his music, writings, performances, as well as Gypsy Hill Music.

News

  • On America’s 250th birthday, Oscar Brand’s 1972 bestseller, Songs of ’76: A Folksinger’s History of the Revolution, has just been reissued. Over 60 songs, with words and guitar accompaniment, all fresh and mostly unfamiliar, are accompanied by Oscar's delightfully personalized historic commentary.

  • The new Chimes of Freedom: Patriotism, Protest, and the Power of Song exhibit at the Folk Americana Roots Hall of Fame features Oscar Brand’s 1972 bestseller, Songs of ’76: A Folksinger’s History of the Revolution.

  • TRO Essex's 2026 American Independence Day salute features Oscar Brand, "champion of American history whose music helped generations connect with the nation's past."

    View update

  • The new Bruce Springsteen Center for American Music features Oscar Brand's 1972 bestseller, Songs of '76: A Folksinger's History of the Revolution, as the centerpiece of its eponymously named exhibit.

    View update

  • Two biographies of Oscar Brand are being written, one by Tony Gillespie, an American folk music archivist and podcaster, the other by Anthony Cantor, a Canadian historian and documentary producer/director.

  • The acclaimed Netflix mini-series Death by Lightning licensed Oscar Brand's song, "Do They Hang Too Low," for its third episode.

  • In an ongoing exhibition, The Woody Guthrie Center is showcasing items from the recently acquired collection of artifacts once belonging to Oscar Brand.
